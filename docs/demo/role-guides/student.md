# Role Guide: Student

This guide outlines the system walkthrough for a Student account.

---

## 1. First Login Experience

* **Landing Interface**: Logs into the Student Learning Portal (`/dashboard`).
* **Visual Widgets**:
  * **Next Up Countdown**: Visual timer showing minutes remaining until their next live class starts.
  * **Grade Tracker Card**: Progress ring showing overall grade point average.
  * **Recent Recordings Shelf**: List of published class recordings they have access to watch.
  * **Task List**: List of upcoming quiz deadlines and pending homework assignments.

---

## 2. Navigation Tour

* **Step 1: Student Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Check homework deadlines, grades, and upcoming sessions.
* **Step 2: Course List (`/academic/courses`)**: Search or browse their enrolled courses.
* **Step 3: Assignments List (`/academic/classes/:id`)**: Select a class to check active assignments and submit work.
* **Step 4: Billing Portal (`/finance/invoices/:id`)**: Check tuition fee statements and input payment details.
* **Step 5: Recordings Player (`/recordings/:token`)**: Stream lecture recordings and view bookmarks.

---

## 3. Typical Daily Workflow

1. Log in to check if a live class is active (joining via the "Live Now" banner).
2. Participate in WebRTC video calls, chat, collaborate on the whiteboard, and play games launched by the teacher.
3. Review recorded lectures from past sessions to study.
4. Complete and submit pending homework assignments before the due date.
5. Take published online quizzes and review graded scores and teacher feedback.
6. Check due tuition fees and pay outstanding invoices.

---

## 4. Permissions Summary

### Things They Can Create
* Assignment submissions (text or files).
* Assessment attempts (initiating a quiz).
* Student answers to individual questions.

### Things They Can Edit
* Assignment submissions (before grading).
* Personal profile details.

### Things They Can View
* Enrolled courses, classes, schedules.
* Personal grades and teacher comments.
* Personal tuition invoices.
* Permitted class recordings.

### Things They Cannot Access
* Ledger and expense panels.
* Other students' submissions, quiz answers, or grades.
* Attendance update tools.
* Classroom creation or scheduling interfaces.
